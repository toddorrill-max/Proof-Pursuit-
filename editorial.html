04 / BUY WITH CONFIDENCE

Protect your budget.
Trust your instincts.

A rare label is never a reason to rush.
Build confidence before you commit.

◇
01Observe before you buy

Take time to learn the community and compare prices. Start with established, licensed retailers and verify the source independently.

02Look beyond the photograph

A dated bottle photo can add context, but it cannot prove authenticity or trustworthiness. Verify the seller’s identity and retailer details separately.

03Check the track record

Look for consistent history and independent references. Pressure to pay immediately or use an irreversible payment is a reason to stop.

04Give the hype a cooling-off period

Try a personal 14–30 day pause after a release. Recheck prices and availability before deciding whether the bottle still fits your budget.
